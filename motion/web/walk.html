Walking speed test

Your phone times a short walk at your usual pace. It measures walking speed only and does not diagnose any illness.

Before you start

  1. Use your usual walking aid or brace. If you might fall, have someone walk beside you. Stop if you feel unsteady.
  2. Mark a start line and a finish line on a straight, flat path.
  3. Stand at the start line with the phone in place.
  4. Tap Start. If the phone goes in your pocket, put it there now. Then stand still until GO.
  5. At GO, walk at your usual pace. Stop at the finish line.
  6. Stand still until it beeps, about 2 seconds. It stops by itself. If you hear no beep, count to 3 before you touch the phone.

If you start using a new walking aid or brace, tell your doctor. The comparison has to start again.

On iPhone, turn Silent mode off and turn the volume up. The phone beeps at GO, and again when it stops timing.
